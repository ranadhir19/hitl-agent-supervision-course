# Benchmark — how this course is scored

This course claims to retrain experienced domain professionals into agent
supervisors — people who can design, supervise, govern, and answer for
autonomous AI agents. Claims are cheap; this file is the measuring instrument.

The scorecard adapts Quality Matters (certification at ≥85% of points **and**
all Essential standards met), the Achieve OER rubrics, the worked-example
effect, deliberate-practice research, and Mayer's segmenting/signalling
principles.

**The bar: ≥85% of total points AND full marks on dimensions 1, 8, and 9
(the Essentials) — scored per module-block by a fresh-eyes reviewer before
that block publishes. Scores are published here, including the misses.**

| # | Dimension | What is measured | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Quality / Accuracy *(Essential)* | Claims traced to primary sources and the named frameworks (NIST AI Risk Management Framework, ISO/IEC 42001, the EU AI Act, the OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications); code and labs run; formulas and figures verified | Full marks |
| 2 | Depth | All ten modules taught at advanced level — the design's exercises and labs in full, not summarised | Modules 1–10 all at advanced |
| 3 | Explanation | Worked examples before theory; a whiteboard derivation per topic | Every topic has both |
| 4 | Engagement | Retention and completion against the channel's own course baseline (measured relatively and honestly — no public YouTube benchmark exists) | Top-quartile vs own baseline |
| 5 | First-principles fundamentality | Each topic derived from the five pillars (autonomy, observability, authority, intervention, accountability) and the three dimensions, not tool-of-the-month | 100% of topics mapped to pillars |
| 6 | Problem complexity | Exercises include ambiguity, injected failures, conflicting instructions, cascades | Every capstone has ≥2 injected failures |
| 7 | Ease of learning | Dependency order respected; prerequisites stated per lesson; the simulation escalates one capability at a time | Zero forward references |
| 8 | Practice & Assessment *(Essential)* | An exercise per topic with a rubric; capstones graded on the published assessment framework | Full marks |
| 9 | Transfer to the new role *(Essential)* | Capstone deliverables identical to real supervisor artefacts (approval matrix, risk assessment, incident record, governance charter) | Full marks |
| 10 | Production & packaging | Channel certification gates passed for every lesson (packaging gate, heard-audio verification, movement check) | Every lesson certified |
